The Voter's Roadmap

Six steps to ensure your voice is heard correctly.

1

Eligibility

Confirm you are a citizen, meet age requirements, and check residency rules in your specific district.

2

Registration

The most critical step. Ensure you are on the voter rolls before the deadline (usually 30 days prior).

3

Research

Look beyond the ads. Study non-partisan guides for candidates and local ballot measures.

4

Voting Method

Decide between Mail-in, Early In-Person, or Election Day. Each has different deadlines.

5

ID Requirements

Does your state require a photo ID? Check now to avoid surprises at the polling station.

6

Vote!

Cast your ballot. Know your rights, like the right to vote if you're in line before polls close.
